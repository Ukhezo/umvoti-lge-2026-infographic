A3 portrait · single column on 64 px (17 mm) margins · numbered sections divided by heavy rules · reading order from top to bottom, from largest and boldest to smallest and lightest
1Masthead
White on navy, with the place name at 152 px. The gold ballot X is the series motif.
2Turnout
A 100-square waffle makes 56.4% physical. The hero number is the second-largest type on the page.
3Share of the vote
Flat bars sorted from highest to lowest. Labels sit at the end of each bar, so there's no legend to decode.
4The new council
A parliament-style arc shows all 27 seats at once. The table and a gold "hung council" label explain it.
5Source and credit
The smallest type, read last: source, date, designer credit and a "not official" note.
